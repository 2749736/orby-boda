OUR STORY

Our story started fourteen years ago in Calgary with bubble tea, wings, and late-night Skype calls, but it quickly grew into a lifelong adventure. From growing up together, working hard, and supporting each other’s biggest dreams. To living abroad in Lisbon and raising our best boy, Melo. We’ve loved every step of our journey together and have been inseparable ever since.

On November 16, 2024, Ormhel popped the big question in El Nido, Palawan, Philippines. To honour the blending of our two cultures, we can’t wait to welcome you to El Salvador as we tie the knot, celebrate fifteen years together, and kick off our next great chapter as husband and wife!

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before the wedding

Can I bring a guest or partner?

We wish we could invite everyone! However, to maintain an intimate atmosphere for our destination wedding, our guest list is strictly limited. Plus ones are exclusively extended to those whose invitations explicitly mention a guest.

Are children invited?

Yes! A celebration isn't a celebration without them. To make things easy for parents, we'll be providing early meal times, special kids' meals, fun activities to keep them entertained, and an on-site attendant so you can enjoy the evening knowing they're in great hands.

Will transportation be provided?

Yes. Transportation will be available from select nearby hotels on the wedding day. We'll share additional details and schedule specifics closer to the date!

What will the weather be like?

November in El Salvador marks the beginning of the dry season, so you can expect warm, sunny days (around 27°C–30°C) with clear skies and cooler, comfortable evenings.

How do I get around?

Getting around is easy! Uber is readily available throughout the area for personal excursions, dining, and exploring. Local taxis can also be arranged directly through your hotel front desk, or you can rent a car if you prefer the flexibility of driving yourself.

What is the currency in El Salvador?

The official currency of El Salvador is the US Dollar ($ USD). Carrying cash (especially smaller bills) is very helpful for small local vendors, though major credit cards are widely accepted at most hotels, restaurants, and shops.

Do I need a travel Visa?

Most travelers (including passport holders from the US, Canada, EU, and UK) do not need a pre-arranged visa for short stays. However, upon arrival at the airport or border, you will need to purchase a Tourist Card (approx. $12 USD) at immigration. Be sure your passport is valid for at least 6 months from your travel dates!
