TRAVEL

Nearest Airport

El Salvador Airport (SAL)

The El Salvador International Airport, San Óscar Arnulfo Romero International Airport (SAL), is located 45 minutes from the capital city, San Salvador. Guests are asked to arrange their airfare independently. We recommend booking early to secure your preferred routes and times.

We recommend staying in San Salvador during the wedding events, as it is a 30-minute drive from the wedding venue and a great central hub for getting around.

Getting to the Wedding Venue

Foret, Comasagua

Foret is located in the Comasagua mountainside near the capital city of San Salvador. To make travelling seamless, complimentary round-trip shuttle transportation will be provided for all official wedding events (pick-up and drop-off points will be centralized). A detailed shuttle timetable will be shared closer to the date.

If you plan to rent a car, complimentary parking is available at the venue, and driving is a lovely way to explore the surrounding villages and mountainside.

THINGS TO DO

If you’re making a holiday of the celebrations, we’ve rounded up a few of our favourite spots in El Salvador. From beautiful beaches to unforgettable sunsets, these are the places we’d most recommend exploring during your stay.

Swim at the Taminique Waterfalls

Tamanique, El Salvador

Nestled in the lush jungle near the coast, this scenic spot offers a refreshing trek leading directly to natural freshwater pools. It’s the ultimate tropical adventure for anyone looking to cool off with a dip in crystal-clear waterfalls.

Hike the Santa Ana Volcano

Santa Ana, El Salvador

Embark on a memorable trek up El Salvador’s highest volcano to witness its breathtaking, turquoise crater lake. The rewarding summit views stretching across the surrounding cloud forests make every step worth it.

Explore the Tazumal Ruins

Chalchuapa, El Salvador

Step back in time at one of the country's most impressive and well-preserved Maya archaeological ruins. Located in Chalchuapa, this historic landmark offers a fascinating glimpse into ancient architecture and pre-Columbian culture.

Zipline the Apaneca Canopy

Apaneca, El Salvador

Fly through the lush treetops of the Ruta de las Flores on an exhilarating zipline canopy adventure. You'll soak in panoramic views of rolling coffee plantations and forested mountain valleys as you glide from platform to platform.

Beach at Playa Costa del Sol

La Paz, El Salvador

Unwind along a wide, palm-fringed stretch of dark sand known for its relaxing ocean tides and seaside restaurants. It is an ideal coastal getaway for spending an afternoon lounging by the water or watching a golden Pacific sunset.
